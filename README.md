### About me

Full Stack Developer with **3+ years** and **20+ production platforms** across e-commerce, travel, healthcare, rental, cargo and mobility — shipped both as long-lived products and as client deliveries.

I work across the whole stack, but the centre of gravity is the backend: MongoDB schemas, Node.js and NestJS APIs, payment and settlement flows, and the cron schedulers that keep everything moving while nobody is watching. On the other side I build the React and Next.js admin panels that sit on top, and in between I own the AWS deploy — EC2, Nginx, PM2, SSL.

- 🏢 Currently a Full Stack Developer at **Geeksynergy Technologies**, Bengaluru
- 💳 Shipped integrations with **4 payment gateways** — Razorpay, Cashfree (EasySplit), Stripe, Easebuzz
- ⚙️ Comfortable owning scheduler-driven workflows, webhook reconciliation and production incidents
- 🎓 Doing an **MCA at Manipal University (Online)** alongside full-time work
- 🌱 Going deeper on distributed system design and AI-assisted product features
- 📫 Reach me at **mhdminhal44@gmail.com**
- ⚡ Football, books, games, and problems where correctness and uptime both have to hold
